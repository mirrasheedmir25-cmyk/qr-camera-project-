QR Camera Preview

Scan the QR code to open this page, then choose whether to allow camera access.

1. Open this page by QR

After publishing this page over HTTPS, paste its public URL here to create a QR code.

QR generation uses an online QR image service. Do not enter private or secret URLs.

2. Camera live preview

Tap Start Camera. Your browser will ask for permission if needed.

📷

Camera preview will appear here after permission is granted.
Status: Camera is off.

Privacy: this demo only displays the camera locally in this browser. It does not take photos, record video, or send camera footage to you or a server.

How to use on Android

  1. Upload this file as index.html to a GitHub repository.
  2. Enable GitHub Pages and wait for the HTTPS website URL.
  3. Paste that URL above and tap Create QR Code.
  4. Share the QR code. The other person scans it and chooses whether to allow camera access.

Camera access generally requires HTTPS or localhost. If permission was denied earlier, open the browser's site settings and change Camera permission.

Camera starts only after a user action and browser permission.
